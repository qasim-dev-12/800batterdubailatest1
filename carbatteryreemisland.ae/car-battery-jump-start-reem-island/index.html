01

What the jump start visit includes

A jump start on its own gets the engine running, but it does not tell you whether the car will start tomorrow. Every visit therefore includes a battery and alternator health check alongside the boost, so you know why the battery went flat.

The AED 99 covers the visit, the jump start and the check. If the battery was simply drained, for example by a light left on or a long spell parked, that is the whole visit and there is nothing more to pay.

  • Boost from a portable jump pack, so no second car is needed in the bay.
  • Battery state of charge and condition checked.
  • Alternator and charging output checked with the engine running.
  • Terminals and connections inspected for corrosion or looseness.
02

If the battery has failed

Sometimes the check shows the battery cannot hold a charge, or the car starts but the battery fails a load test. In that case the technician gives you a quote for a compatible replacement on the spot. You decide whether to go ahead; there is no obligation to buy.

If you go ahead, the battery can usually be fitted in the same visit. Fitted batteries start from AED 249, and the quote covers the battery, standard fitting, test, old battery removal for recycling and the warranty for the brand you choose.

03

Basement and tower car parks

Many Dubai towers have basement or podium parking with access control, low ceilings and tight bays. A portable jump pack works in these spaces without moving the car. Send the building name, parking level, bay number and any visitor or security arrangements so the technician can get to the car directly.

If someone else has the key, such as a family member, driver or colleague, they can meet the technician while you approve by phone or WhatsApp.

04

Roadside and mall parking

At the kerb or in a mall car park such as Reem Mall, share a pinned or live location on WhatsApp and add the road name, nearest landmark and which side of the road you are on. Switch on the hazard lights and, if it is safe, wait away from traffic.

If the car is somewhere unsafe to work, tell us when you message. We can advise on moving it or arranging recovery.

05

Why a jump start may not last

A boost puts enough energy in the battery to crank the engine, not to fully recharge it. If the battery is old or damaged by heat, it may go flat again after the next stop, even if the drive home was fine. That is why the health check matters more than the boost itself.

A short drive after a jump rarely restores a deeply drained battery. If the car has been parked for weeks, or the battery is several summers old, a full test is worth doing before you rely on it.

Old battery

UAE heat shortens battery life, so a battery a few summers old may not recover.

Deep discharge

A battery left flat for days can lose capacity permanently.

Charging fault

If the alternator is not charging, the battery drains again while you drive.

Parasitic drain

An accessory or fault drawing power while parked flattens the battery overnight.
