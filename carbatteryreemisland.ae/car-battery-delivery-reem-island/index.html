02

How to confirm the right battery

A battery that does not match the car is the most common problem with delivery orders, so we check before anything leaves. The easiest way is to send a clear photo of the label on your current battery, along with the car's make, model, year and engine.

We match the size, terminal layout, capacity and battery type. If your car has stop-start, the type matters as much as the size: an AGM car needs an AGM battery, and an EFB car needs EFB or an AGM upgrade.

  • A photo of the current battery label
  • Make, model, year and engine size
  • Whether the car has stop-start
  • Your building and where you want the battery handed over
03

Delivery timing and old battery collection

We deliver to residential towers, office buildings and other addresses across Reem Island, including Shams Abu Dhabi, Marina Square, City of Lights, Najmat Abu Dhabi, Makers District and around Reem Mall. Tell us the building, the entrance to use and who will receive the battery.

The old battery is collected free when the new one is handed over, so have it ready if you have already removed it. Old batteries go for recycling.

04

Warranty registration and payment

The warranty is registered in your name at delivery. Its length depends on the brand: 24 months on Bosch, Varta, Panasonic and Mobis, 18 months on Amaron, ACDelco and Energizer, and 12 months on value brands. Keep the warranty details we give you in case you need to make a claim.

Payment is taken at the door by cash, card, Tabby or Tamara. Tell us when you order if you would like to split the payment.

05

When to choose fitting instead

Some cars are better left to a technician. Many BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Volkswagen, Range Rover and Porsche models, and other stop-start cars, need the new battery registered or coded at the car, and fitting without it can lead to charging problems and warning lights. Some batteries also sit under a seat or in the boot with venting that must be refitted correctly.

If the car is not starting and you are not sure the battery is the cause, a test first can save you buying a battery you do not need. For any of these cases, the mobile fitting service tests the battery and charging system and fits the battery where the car is parked.

Choose delivery

You know the exact battery, the car does not need registration and you are comfortable fitting it safely.

Choose fitting

Your car needs registration or coding, the battery is hard to reach, or you want the battery and charging system tested first.
