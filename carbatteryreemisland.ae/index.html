Stop-start and European cars

Stop-start, AGM and European cars

A car with stop-start or regenerative braking was built for an EFB or AGM battery, and a standard battery fails early in it. The technician checks the battery type already in the car before matching the replacement.

AGM and EFB, matched

If the car came with AGM, it gets AGM. EFB can be upgraded to AGM; it is never downgraded to a standard battery.

Registration and coding

BMW, many Mercedes-Benz and Audi models and Range Rover track battery age and capacity. The new battery is registered to the car where it requires it. Confirm your model when you book.

Battery care

Looking after a battery in a tower car park

Cars on Reem Island often sit for days in a basement bay and then make short trips inside the island. That pattern is hard on a battery. A few habits make a flat battery less likely.

Before summer travel

A car left parked for weeks keeps drawing power for its alarm and electronics. Book a test before you fly, or arrange for someone to start and drive it.

Short trips inside the island

A short drive between towers may not put back what the start used. A longer drive once a week helps the battery recover.

Heat in podium and open bays

Heat speeds up the wear inside a battery. Covered basement bays are kinder than open podium levels in summer.

When a test is worth booking

A slow crank, dim lights at idle, stop-start switching itself off or a battery warning are all reasons to test before it fails.
